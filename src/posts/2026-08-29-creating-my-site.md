Last night I was putting the finishing touches on the MVP release of the front door application for my NFL prediction model.  Goal Line$.  It's beautiful, almost makes up for the fact that the model is not that accurate.  As I was about to buy my next domain, I realized that maybe, just maybe, it was time to finally stop spending money and buy that one personal domain and move everything underneath.  So I started as I always do, imagining names with ChatGPT. There were some really good ones, she knows me.  Marjorie Made This.  Marjorie Was Here.

And then I saw it.  **Marjorie Has Ideas.**

This was the one.  I needed this domain.  I do have ideas, lots of ideas.  They aren't all good ideas, but some lead to some pretty interesting things.  I like to learn and explore hands on, to see what happens when ideas become real.  Turns out, I also like apps built for me, by me, meeting my needs.

Here are the things that came from those ideas and some of the backstory behind them.  Some are in production, some are in progress, some are still just ideas waiting for the time, energy, and spark to get started.
