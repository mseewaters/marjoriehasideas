### These things scream "made by AI", lets try to minimize them

For the kind of personal/project website you have, my AI-slop detector goes off at:

1. Warm beige/off-white background + charcoal text. The current king of tasteful AI aesthetics. Unfortunately, also genuinely attractive.
2. Elegant oversized serif headings paired with a clean sans-serif body font. Bonus AI points for italics.
3. Huge amounts of immaculate whitespace. Everything breathing so luxuriously that three paragraphs require six screens.
4. Perfectly centered hero section. Tiny icon/avatar → enormous name → clever italic tagline → generous gap → intro.
5. The clever two-line tagline.
   Some are useful. Some are ridiculous.
   A surprising number become websites.
   Yours absolutely fits the pattern. I still vote keep it, because it's good and sounds like you.
6. Pill-shaped tags everywhere. in progress personal experiment AI in little rounded capsules.
7. Rounded rectangular project cards. Particularly three equally sized ones in a perfect grid with subtle borders/shadows.
8. Tiny eyebrow headings. ABOUT or SELECTED WORK in uppercase, letter-spaced sans serif above a giant serif heading.
9. The gradient blob. Purple/blue/orange fuzzy thing floating behind text. Immediate jail.
10. Generic abstract line art. Especially a tasteful squiggle whose primary purpose is proving that the page has "visual interest."
11. Every section having the exact same rhythm. Heading → 2 sentences → cards → enormous whitespace → heading → 2 sentences → cards.
12. Overly polished microcopy. “Exploring the intersection of technology, curiosity, and creativity.” This one is worse than beige. 😂
13. “Building at the intersection of…” Straight to jail.
14. Generic icon sets used decoratively. Little sparkle for creativity, lightning bolt for innovation, brain for AI.
15. Everything having the same visual importance. AI designs are often extremely competent hierarchically but weirdly devoid of eccentricity. Nothing unexpectedly huge, tiny, offset, dense, or strange.
16. A site that could belong to anybody once you replace the name. This is the big one. If I can change “Marjorie” to “Sarah” and change NFL modeling to pottery without disturbing the design, the visual identity isn't doing much work.
